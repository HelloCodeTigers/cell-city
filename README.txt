CELL CITY  -  by CodeTigers
Interactive 3D biology lessons (English + Hindi)

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip this folder anywhere on your computer.
2. Double-click  index.html
   It opens in your web browser (Chrome or Edge works best).
   No internet is needed. Everything is inside this folder.

Keep all files together: index.html needs the "lib" and "fonts" folders next to it.

LESSONS
- Lesson 1: Inside a cell (animal cell)
- Lesson 2: The plant cell
Switch language with the EN / हिं button, or press L on the keyboard.

PUT IT ONLINE FOR FREE (Cloudflare Pages)
1. Create a free account at dash.cloudflare.com
2. Go to  Workers & Pages  ->  Create  ->  Pages  ->  Upload assets
3. Give the project a name (for example: cell-city) and drag this whole folder in.
4. Click Deploy. Your app gets a free web address ending in .pages.dev
